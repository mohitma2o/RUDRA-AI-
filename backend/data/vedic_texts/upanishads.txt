Upanishads - Wisdom of the Self

The Upanishads teach that the true Self (Atman) is one with the Absolute Reality (Brahman).
They emphasize inner inquiry, meditation, and the realization that separation is an illusion.
Real spiritual progress comes from transcending ego-based identity and realizing the unity of all life.

Practice discrimination between the eternal and the temporary, cultivate discipline, and live with humility.
True knowledge arises when the mind is quiet and the heart opens to the inner light.
