Bhagavad Gita - Key Spiritual Guidance

The Bhagavad Gita describes the path of duty (karma), devotion (bhakti), and wisdom (jnana).
It teaches that one should act without attachment to the results, offering work as service to the Divine.
The text encourages equanimity in success and failure, mastery of the mind, and selfless love.

Pursue dharma with calmness, remain steady in joy and sorrow, and remember the eternal Self beyond the body.
Serve others, study the self, and use spiritual practice to remain centered in the face of challenges.
