Mahabharata - Duty, Choice, and Consequence

The Mahabharata teaches the complexity of dharma and the power of choice.
It highlights the importance of acting rightly even when the situation is uncertain or difficult.
The story of Arjuna on the battlefield shows that wisdom and courage arise when duty is pursued with clarity.

Balance noble intent with careful discernment, and seek guidance from tradition while remaining true to your own conscience.
The Mahabharata warns that attachment, envy, and confusion create suffering, while self-discipline brings freedom.
