Ramayana - Righteous Action and Devotion

The Ramayana illustrates the ideals of truth, duty, loyalty, and devotion.
Rama is upheld as the righteous king, Sita as purity and devotion, and Hanuman as the devoted servant.
The story encourages surrender to dharma, courage in adversity, and steadfastness to truth.

When facing hardship, remember that service, compassion, and moral resolve lead to inner victory.
The text teaches that true strength lies in maintaining virtue and honoring one's responsibilities.
